## 🚀 Flagship — MRK18 · Your AI Chief Marketing Officer

<div align="center">

[![Try it live](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E2%86%92%20Try%20it%20live-mrk18.com-38bdf8?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=0f172a)](https://mrk18.com)

</div>

> **MRK18 is an *Artificially Intelligent Marketing Officer* for founders** — a production web app I designed and shipped end-to-end, from the marketing site to the agentic LLM backend.

A founder drops a **URL** (or just describes an **idea**) and, in ~15 seconds, gets four grounded verdicts — **USP · Competition · Go-To-Market · Ad-Spend Audit** — with **live competitor discovery** and a hard rule: **zero invented numbers**. From there they can **chat with their CMO** about the result, upload an **ad-spend CSV for a forensic audit** (every figure computed server-side, so it's *provable*, never model-guessed), and step into the full product — **Comrk** (the execution desk: strategy → content → approval gates) and **Chief** (the analytics command center that reads your numbers and returns a weekly diagnosis).

**What makes it real engineering, not a wrapper:**

- 🧠 **Agentic LLM pipeline** — orchestrated with **LangGraph**, served on **Groq** (`gpt-oss-120b`, `llama-3.3-70b`) with **Tavily** live web research for grounded, real competitors.
- 🔒 **Provable analytics** — the ad audit does *all* arithmetic in Python, so the CMO can never invent a metric; prompt-injection hardening + strict-JSON contracts throughout.
- ⚡ **Public, no-signup taster** — per-IP daily rate limits, a global budget wall, per-domain caching, and payload versioning to keep a free, abusable LLM endpoint safe and cheap.
- 🔗 **Ad-platform connectors** — Meta & Google via **OAuth 2.1 + PKCE** (Login for Business `config_id`).
- 🎛️ **Server-side entitlement gates + a frontend demo mode** — non-members explore Comrk/Chief on sample data while the paid backend stays untouched.
- 📊 **Data-dense, theme-aware UI** — Recharts dashboards, a scroll-driven 3D device showcase, light/dark, and a fixed-bottom CMO chat.
